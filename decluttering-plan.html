Week 4 — The Basement

0%
This week is different. A lot of what's down there is your mother's — and that's not the same task as sorting a linen closet. Go in this order deliberately: easy, non-sentimental stuff first to build momentum, then the harder boxes once you've got a rhythm. It's fine to keep more here than you would elsewhere, to go slower than planned, or to ask a sibling or someone close to your mother to go through boxes with you. If a week isn't enough, let it take two — nothing above resets or depends on finishing this on schedule.

Keeping it that way

  1. One in, one out. When something new comes in, something existing leaves.
  2. 12-month rule. If it wasn't used or worn in the past year and isn't seasonal/sentimental, it goes.
  3. A home for everything. If an item doesn't have a designated spot, it's a candidate to leave.
  4. Mail & paper — daily. Sort as it arrives; recycle immediately, don't stockpile.
  5. Quarterly mini-pass. Spend one afternoon every 3 months re-checking closets and the garage before clutter rebuilds.
